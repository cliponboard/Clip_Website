Clip partnered with China Airlines and Taiwanese design brand JIA to develop new Business Class tableware collection inspired by the warmth of home, featuring over 30 porcelain pieces with jade-green gradients and ink-wash-style mountain motifs, including signature designs like a gourd-shaped butter dish doubling as a chopstick rest, lantern-shaped salt and pepper shakers, and a propeller-inspired multifunctional utensil.

China Airlines, Taiwan’s leading carrier, has unveiled its new Business Class tableware collection, bringing together Taiwanese cultural references, contemporary design, hospitality and sustainability.

China Airlines has once again partnered with JIA, the award-winning Taiwanese design brand. Developed around the concept of Home, the collection draws inspiration from the warmth and sense of reunion associated with traditional lanterns. Their soft, rounded forms are translated into the gentle curves of the tableware, creating a welcoming and familiar dining experience on board.

The porcelain features subtle gradients of China Airlines’ signature Sustainable Jade, combined with grayscale mountain landscapes. Reminiscent of traditional ink-wash painting, the composition brings a sense of calm and serenity to the cabin and evokes Taiwan’s natural landscape.

The new Premium Business Class and Business Class collection comprises more than 30 pieces, including main plates, multipurpose dishes, stainless-steel cutlery and glassware. Inspired by the traditional Japanese lunch box, the Swiss Bento brings smart design and lasting sustainability to every meal aboard SWISS.

Clip is excited to unveil the latest version of the Swiss Bento, a sophisticated and innovative food serving box designed for use onboard. Inspired by traditional Japanese lunch boxes, the Swiss Bento is utilized by Swiss International Air Lines for second service, breakfast, and light meals.

Passengers are impressed by its premium, smooth black finish and the intuitive design, with the cutlery cleverly integrated into the lid for ease of use. They also appreciate its practicality, as all components can be neatly stored back inside the box once the meal is finished. This streamlined design enhances the efficiency of both meal delivery and collection, allowing cabin crew to focus more on personalized interactions with passengers.

Designed with sustainability in mind, the Swiss Bento eliminates the need for disposable lids or protective films, as the food is directly stored inside the box. Its compact design is highly efficient, allowing 12 boxes to fit comfortably in a single standard drawer, which helps optimize valuable galley space—an ever-pressing concern as new aircraft feature smaller galleys.

Moreover, the Swiss Bento is built to last, enduring at least 100 cycles of filling, shipping, eating, cleaning, and storage. This durable design is a testament to Swiss International Air Lines’ commitment to reducing the ecological impact of air travel.

A truly modular headphone, designed to be disassembled and reassembled in under a minute without any tools.

Flydubai has just introduced a radically new noise cancelling headphone in the business class cabin, with the goal of dramatically reducing the carbon footprint of this specific piece of equipment.

The new concept makes it possible to separate the headphones into four main modules: Headband with ear cups, Electronic modules, Cables and Earpads.

The disassembly and assembly process is very simple, and can be achieved in a minute without requiring tools. Cabin crew and maintenance teams can easily execute the process with only a few minutes of training.

Years of experience have concluded that most of the headphone defects on airplanes are due to broken plastic components or defective cables, usually resulting in the whole device being discarded.

This new modular design allows airlines to replace the defective parts of each headset and reuse the remaining parts of the headphones to regenerate new equipment.

Airlines can reduce carbon emissions and long-run budget by 50% or more with this new design.
